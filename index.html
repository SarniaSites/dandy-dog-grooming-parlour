What we do

Every coat is groomed differently. Pick the one closest to your dog's.

Clip and scissor

Curly coats mat quickly if they're left. We bath, dry and fluff the coat straight, then clip and scissor it to the length and shape you ask for.

  • Bath and blow-dry
  • Coat brushed through and fluffed straight
  • Clipped and scissored to your length
  • Face, feet and tail tidied
Book a curly coat groom

Bath and de-shed

Thick double coats hold a dense undercoat. A de-shed bath and a long blow-dry lift the loose coat out, so less of it ends up on your sofa.

  • De-shed bath
  • Long blow-dry to loosen the undercoat
  • Undercoat brushed out
  • Feet and trousers tidied
Book a de-shed

Brush-out and trim

A long coat can stay long if it's kept brushed out, or come shorter for easier care between visits. Your call.

  • Bath and blow-dry
  • Tangles and knots worked out
  • Trimmed to the length you want
  • Eyes and feet kept clear
Book a long coat groom

A gentle first visit

A short visit for young dogs: a warm bath, a dry and plenty of fuss, so the parlour feels familiar before their first full groom. The first bath is free.

  • Warm bath and rinse
  • Towel and gentle blow-dry
  • Time on the table, at their pace

How a groom runs

Four stages, from scruffy on arrival to ready for collection. Tap a stage to see it.

  1. 1

    Bath

    Warm water and a proper rinse, right down to the skin. Thick double coats get a de-shed wash.

  2. 2

    Dry

    Blow-dried and brushed through. On a thick coat this is where the loose undercoat comes away.

  3. 3

    Clip and scissor

    Clipped and scissored to the shape you asked for, with the face, feet and tail tidied by hand.

  4. 4

    Ready to collect

    Finished, fresh and looking very dandy. Book the next one before you go.
